# Backtest: 2023 Q1 – 2026 Q1 (13 quarters)

30,873 insider buys and 103,281 sells on listed companies with 6 months of prices afterwards
(1,874 trades held for review were left out). Each move is measured from the first close after
the filing date. Reproduce with `scripts/backtest/pool.py` (see `scripts/backtest/README.md`).

**Main measure:** "rose" = share of buys where the stock was higher 6 months later. Baseline: **57%** of all buys.
"Stable" = number of quarters where the group's median beat the all-buys median.

Today the score runs backwards. With the proposed weights it runs the right way, and the notable list beat the
old one in 10 of 13 quarters.

What it means in practice: notable becomes an officer or director buying an S&P 500 stock, or a CEO buying at a
top-200 company. About 113 notable trades a quarter (~9 a week) instead of ~290. Users still get every alert for what they star.

## Caveats
- **Survivorship:** the company list is today's, so stocks delisted since 2023 are missing. Delisted stocks are often losers,
  so absolute numbers are too rosy; comparisons between groups are less affected.
- **Bull market:** 2023–2025 were mostly rising years, so "rose" is high for everyone. Compare groups, not with 50%.
- **The edge is modest:** 57% → 62–65%. Never imply returns in marketing.
- Several buys by the same person or company count separately, so the effective sample is smaller than the counts.
