# Backtest: Q1 2026 (one quarter only — directional, not conclusive)

11,859 insider buys and sells on listed companies (2,378 buys), Jan–Mar 2026.
Each move is measured from the first close after the filing date (when a user could act on the alert).

## What it suggests (both views agree; still to confirm over more quarters)
- **The current score picked worse trades than average.** Its size and CEO points pointed the wrong way.
- **Buys at the top-200 S&P companies did best** (82–89% higher at 6 months), but the samples are small (19 and 33).
  S&P ranks 201–500 behaved like small caps.
- **Directors and other officers beat CEOs.** 10% owners did worst.
- **Sells barely predict direction.** Consider scoring sells lower overall.
- **The proposed cluster rule removes noise without losing signal.** Clusters have only a slight edge.
- **Never imply returns in marketing.** The pattern is weak outside the biggest companies.
