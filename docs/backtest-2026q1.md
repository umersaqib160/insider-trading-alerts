# Backtest: Q1 2026 (one quarter only — directional, not conclusive)

11,859 insider buys and sells on listed companies (2,378 buys), Jan–Mar 2026.
The move is measured from the first close after each filing date, against the S&P 500 for S&P
members and against the Russell 2000 for everyone else. "Beat" = share of trades where the stock
did better than its benchmark.

| Insider buys | n | 3 mo median vs benchmark | 3 mo beat | 6 mo median vs benchmark | 6 mo beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All buys (baseline) | 2,378 | −9.2% | 29% | −9.8% | 34% |
| Notable, current rules | 289 | −14.1% | 22% | −12.5% | 33% |
| Notable, proposed rules | 185 | −13.7% | 24% | −11.7% | 31% |
| S&P 500 rank 1–100 | 19 | −5.8% | 37% | +2.0% | 53% |
| S&P 500 rank 101–200 | 33 | −1.2% | 48% | +1.6% | 58% |
| S&P 500 rank 201–500 | 169 | −12.5% | 28% | −9.8% | 34% |
| Outside the S&P 500 | 2,157 | −9.0% | 29% | −10.2% | 33% |
| CEO / CFO / Chair | 512 | −12.9% | 24% | −13.9% | 30% |
| Directors | 1,029 | −6.6% | 34% | −5.7% | 40% |
| 10% owners only | 416 | −10.6% | 28% | −18.2% | 26% |
| $1M+ | 302 | −13.8% | 25% | −13.2% | 31% |
| Cluster, kept by proposed rule | 414 | −9.9% | 30% | −8.4% | 40% |
| Cluster, old rule only (token buys or funds) | 313 | −10.0% | 26% | −7.7% | 36% |

Sells: stocks with insider sales trailed their benchmark at 6 months 58–61% of the time, and
pre-planned (10b5-1) and unplanned sales behaved about the same.

What it suggests, still to be confirmed over more quarters:
- **The current score picked worse trades than average.** Its size and CEO points pointed the wrong way.
- **Buys at the top-200 S&P companies were the only groups that beat their benchmark** at 6 months.
  These samples are small (19 and 33). S&P ranks 201–500 behaved like small caps.
- **Directors beat CEOs**, and 10% owners did worst.
- **The proposed cluster rule removes noise without losing signal.** Clusters have only a slight edge.
- **Insider buys in general didn't beat the market this quarter.** Never imply returns in marketing.
