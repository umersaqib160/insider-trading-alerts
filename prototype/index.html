What you get alerted about

Applies to every company and politician you star.

  • Buys Buy

    Open-market purchases by company insiders (Form 4 code P) and purchases disclosed by politicians.

  • Sells Sell

    Open-market sales by company insiders (Form 4 code S) and full or partial sales disclosed by politicians.

  • Unverified forum signals Unverified

    Reddit and StockTwits posts that mention your starred stocks or politicians. These are claims, not filings, and are always labelled that way.

Stock grants, option exercises and shares withheld for tax are skipped. Buys and sells can't be turned off yet.
